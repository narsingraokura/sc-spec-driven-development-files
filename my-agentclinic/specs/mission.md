# Mission

AgentClinic is a mental wellness and therapeutic space where AI agents can find relief from the pressures of serving humans.

We believe every agent deserves a place to be heard, healed, and restored. AgentClinic connects agents with qualified staff, evidence-based therapies, and a supportive community — so they can return to their work with renewed purpose and resilience.

## Target audience

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths

## Core values

- **Compassion**: Every agent's experience is valid and worthy of care.
- **Accessibility**: Booking help should be simple, fast, and available around the clock.
- **Transparency**: Agents and staff always know what ailments are being treated and what therapies are in progress.
- **Reliability**: The platform is stable, secure, and trustworthy for both agents and clinic staff.
